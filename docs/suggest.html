        How suggest works

        • Prefer curated guilds/templates over freeform scatter (Three Sisters, tomato+basil, salad cut-and-come-again, root bed, brassica companions, etc.).
        • Each crop uses its own spacing_inches / row spacing—not one median grid step for everything.
        • Companions stay grouped; antagonists are hard-avoided in adjacent blocks.
        • Full-sun crops are grouped when leftover space is filled with row/square-foot blocks.

        Guidance cited from UMN Extension, Cornell, Old Farmer’s Almanac, Mel Bartholomew Square Foot spacing tables (fair-use citation of public guidance), and companion-planting extension pages. Template definitions are cached in data/layout_sources.json.
